Why do we remember?

Remembrance Day began after the First World War ended on November 11, 1918. Millions of people had died, and many more had been hurt or lost their homes.

Every year on November 11, we remember the people who suffered and died because of war.

We wear poppies as a symbol of remembrance.

Remembrance Day is a time to think about peace, kindness, and the importance of solving problems without violence.
