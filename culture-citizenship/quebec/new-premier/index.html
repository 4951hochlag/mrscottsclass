Who is PSPP?

Paul St-Pierre Plamondon is Québec's new premier. People often call him PSPP. He is the leader of the Parti Québécois (PQ).

He became premier after the October 5, 2026 election. The premier helps lead Québec's government and make important decisions.

What does he want to do?

  • Protect French: Help keep French an important language in Québec.
  • Help people: Support people who need housing or food.
  • Improve schools: Make education better.
  • Save money: Reduce government waste.
  • Change immigration: Reduce the number of new immigrants coming to Québec.
  • Québec independence: He wants Québec to become its own country, separate from Canada.

These are his goals and plans. They have not all happened yet. People have different opinions about these ideas.
