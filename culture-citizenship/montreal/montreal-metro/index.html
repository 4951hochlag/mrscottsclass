🚇 Montreal Metro

The Montreal Metro is an underground train system that helps people travel around Montreal. It opened in 1966 and has four lines: Green, Orange, Yellow, and Blue.

Each line has stations where passengers can get on and off the trains. People use the Metro to go to school, work, shopping, and other places around the city.

The Metro is operated by the Société de transport de Montréal (STM). It is an important part of Montreal's public transportation system.
