Four in a Row: Fractions to Percentages

How to play: Two players take turns. Choose any available fraction and convert it to a percentage. A correct answer claims that square in your color. An incorrect answer ends your turn, and the square stays available. The first player to connect four squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins!
