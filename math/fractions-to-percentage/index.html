A percentage tells us how much of something we have out of 100.

The word percent means “out of 100.”

For example, 25% means 25 out of 100.

A percentage tells us how much of something we have. It is a simple way of saying whether we have a little, a medium amount, or a lot of something. A percentage goes from 0% (none) to 100% (all).
